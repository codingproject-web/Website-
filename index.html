What is Environmental Pollution?

Environmental pollution is the contamination of air, water, and land by harmful substances. It affects human health, wildlife, and natural ecosystems. Reducing pollution is necessary to maintain a healthy planet for future generations.

Causes of Environmental Pollution

  • Smoke released from factories
  • Vehicle exhaust gases
  • Plastic waste and garbage dumping
  • Deforestation

Effects of Pollution on Earth

  1. Global warming
  2. Health problems in humans
  3. Climate change
  4. Loss of biodiversity

Types of Pollution

Type Main Cause Harmful Effect
Air Pollution Vehicle and factory smoke Breathing problems
Water Pollution Industrial waste Death of aquatic life
Land Pollution Plastic and garbage Soil damage

Solutions – 3R Principle

Reduce

Use fewer resources and avoid unnecessary waste.

Reuse

Reuse items instead of throwing them away.

Recycle

Convert waste materials into reusable products.
